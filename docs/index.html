Feasibility quadrant

Capital openness (Chinn-Ito) on x; infrastructure / GDP-per-capita score on y; bubble size and color = composite SOS. Q1 (top-right) flags actionable-now jurisdictions; Q4 (bottom-left) is structurally blocked.

quadrantScatter(Plot, sos, {height: 460})

How to read this

  • Composite SOS rolls up three independent natural experiments — FATF greylisting, FDI-derisked corridors, and EU AMLD harmonization — into a single country-level score. Higher = more exposure to correspondent-banking frictions on complex-goods trade.
  • Three components (FATF / FDI / AMLD) decompose the headline score and identify which channel dominates for each country.
  • Quadrants combine the SOS with two readiness dimensions (capital openness × infrastructure) so an analyst can separate high-need from actionable-now jurisdictions.
